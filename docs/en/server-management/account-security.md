# Securing your game panel account

Use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication if available in your account settings.

1. Open the [game panel](https://panel.gamehoster.fi/) and check its address before signing in.
2. Open **Account Settings** and review the available security options.
3. If enabling two-factor authentication, follow the panel instructions and store recovery codes securely, separate from your authentication device.
4. Give others their own [subuser permissions](./subusers.md) instead of sharing your password.

If you suspect unauthorized access, change the password and review server users and activity. Contact support if you cannot access your account. Do not send passwords or recovery codes to support.
