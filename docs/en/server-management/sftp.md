# Connecting with SFTP

SFTP allows you to transfer files between your computer and your game server securely.

## Requirements

You need:

- An SFTP client, such as FileZilla or WinSCP.
- Your server's SFTP hostname and port.
- Your SFTP login credentials.

## Connecting

1. Open your SFTP client.
2. Enter the hostname and port shown in your server panel.
3. Enter your username and password.
4. Connect to your server.
5. Transfer the files you need.

::: warning
Never share your SFTP credentials with other people.
:::
