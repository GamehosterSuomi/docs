# Getting started with web hosting

Gamehoster's stated Plesk address is [web.gamehoster.fi](https://web.gamehoster.fi/). Before signing in, check that the browser shows a valid secure connection. If it reports a certificate error, contact Gamehoster support before entering your credentials.

## Setup checklist

1. Sign in with your supplied credentials and confirm that the correct subscription and domain are visible.
2. Check the domain's DNS records against the values supplied for your service before changing them. DNS changes can take time to propagate.
3. Find out how to upload site files and where the site's database is hosted. Back up the existing site before replacing files or data.
4. Check that HTTPS works and the certificate covers the site's domain names.
5. Keep your site software and extensions updated according to their vendors' instructions.

Plesk features depend on your plan and permissions. Contact support if a required feature or DNS target is unclear. Do not include passwords or private keys in support requests.

See the [Plesk customer guide](https://docs.plesk.com/en-US/obsidian/customer-guide/quick-start-with-plesk.69259/).
